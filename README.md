## About me

I'm **Pham Trung Truc**, also known as **GenTpham**. My focus is AI engineering: bringing language models, retrieval, and tool-based workflows together in practical applications.

I enjoy understanding how a system works, building it, and improving it through experimentation. Alongside LLM applications, I explore machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and the infrastructure behind AI systems.

**Currently building**  
AI applications with production readiness in mind, with an emphasis on agents, retrieval, and workflow automation.

**Currently learning**  
Multi-agent systems, AI infrastructure, and approaches for connecting models with external knowledge and tools.

**Long-term direction**  
Grow into a senior AI engineering role and contribute to open-source AI projects.
